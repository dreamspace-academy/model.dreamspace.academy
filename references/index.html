A small number of entries, mostly books and institutional reports, carry no stable public link; they are listed with enough detail to be found in a library catalogue.

What a citation here does and does not mean

These references explain the published foundations of the approach. They do not imply that any author or institution listed endorses DreamSpace Academy, reviewed this site, or agrees with how their work has been applied. Where a finding has been taken in a direction its authors did not, the relevant page says so.

Corrections

When a citation, figure or link on this site is corrected, the page’s updated date and the site version change, and a substantive correction is summarised in the release history. Corrections are recorded rather than made silently.

If you find a reference that is misattributed, superseded or misapplied, please tell DreamSpace Academy. Two kinds of error get watched for particularly: statistics superseded by a newer edition of the same national bulletin, and findings quoted at a strength their authors did not claim.
