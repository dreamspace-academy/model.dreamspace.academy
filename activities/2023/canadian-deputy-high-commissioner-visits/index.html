Visit, 2023

The Deputy High Commissioner of Canada visits

Daniel Bood and the Counsellor for Political and Trade Affairs looked at economic-development ventures.

When
5 September 2023
Where
Batticaloa
Format
Diplomatic visit
With
High Commission of Canada

Daniel Bood, Deputy High Commissioner of Canada, visited with the Counsellor for Political and Trade Affairs in September 2023, looking at potential economic-development ventures and going through the labs.

With the High Commissioner’s own visit in April, the Canadian mission sent three senior people here inside eighteen months.
