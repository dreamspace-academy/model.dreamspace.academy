DreamSpace Academy records each activity on its own page: workshops, bootcamps, openings, visits, festivals and relief operations, in the order they happened. New entries are added as they are documented.

The pages about the organisation describe how the work is meant to fit together, and these pages are the record it can be held against. They record what happened, including days where only a date, a place and a photograph are held.

How to read the numbers on these pages

Where a participant count appears, it counts the people in a room on one day, recorded at the time. It does not count separate individuals, and the counts are never added together into a figure for reach. A repeated title means the same format delivered again to a different group, and summing five mornings into one number would overstate the reach.

What the activity record does not yet include

The record runs from the founding in September 2018 to the present and is not complete. It is built from what was documented at the time (photographs, posts, planning packs), so a quiet month means a month nobody wrote down, not a month with nothing in it. Of the 292 activities listed above, 218 carry images and the rest show a placeholder while their pictures are prepared for publication.

Where a page holds only a date and a place, nothing more was recorded. DreamSpace Academy publishes a thin entry that is true in place of a fuller one that is partly invented, and thin entries are the ones most likely to be filled in later. Two entries name a partner that could not be independently confirmed, and say so on the page; the work is still listed.

The unevenness across years is real. 2021 holds three logged deliveries and a launch to the International Space Station; 2024 holds sixty-eight entries. That reflects a pandemic, an economic crisis and a recovery, and it is not smoothed into a growth curve.
